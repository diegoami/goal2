# Image coverage and remaining capture work

The [classified inventory](screen-inventory.md) contains a verified 640×400 representative image for all 29 E015 screen kinds. This checklist tracks significant alternative states and screens outside that classifier. An image counts as covered here only when it appears beside its explanation in a chapter; provenance for E015 and the published E031 shoot-out state is in [image-provenance.tsv](image-provenance.tsv), and single-action G1A captures have per-step hashes in [captures](captures/). These are screens reconstructed from original DOSBox-X savestates, never AI artwork. [confirmed: E015 manifest; E031 save register; M244 capture logs]

| Area | Illustrated observations | Remaining image or fact gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Launch and managers | Load, missing file, new manager count/country/name, Holland loaded hub | More than two managers and complete local handoff; failed or overwritten save alternatives. |
| Management | Hub, full squad and pool player cards, adjacent player, individual and team tactics, captain, substitution take-off/put-on/illegal | Position swap before/after, formation and marking alternatives, injury/suspension card, condition across rounds. |
| Competition | Fixture rows and watch-hint alternatives, qualifying Groups F/G, history, bulletin, round in progress/results | Country team-info card, final tournament and other continent views, later bulletin, winners and year transition. |
| Match | Watch question, pre-match, pitch, commentary, halftime, substitution, full-time, statistics and summary | Managed-side goal action replay/save prompt, corner/goal-kick taker, card/injury notice, extra-time menu and half, shoot-out order/kicks, distinct quick-result outcome. |
| Save/replay | Save name/confirmation, quit, summary view | Saved-goal replay viewer, multiple-save load picker and cancel/overwrite cases. |

The unpictured branches above have evidence in E031 logs or the original help where cited in the corresponding chapter. They are **text-supported, not image-complete**. The no-substitutions-left prompt, F9 pitch meaning, exact tie-break display formula and all multiplayer handoffs also lack sufficient single-step proof. Capture only with a hash-verified original state and record action and before/after hashes; do not infer one key's effect from a multi-key burst. [confirmed: E031 C11–C52 and C68–C71; reviewed F030; evidence gaps]
