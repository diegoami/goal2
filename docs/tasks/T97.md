# T97 The result of a finished match, and Continue

- **Kind**: slice (#262; the last of its first-release slices)
- **Issue**: #262 · **Branch**: `task/T97-result-continue` locally (a cloud session uses its own branch) · **Merge after**: T96
- **Evidence**:
  - [designed: accepted vision #266 screens 08 and 09] Full time: the score, half time, each side's scorers, the original's statistics, man of the match; **Continue**, **Round results**.
  - [confirmed: `watched_schema` RESULT_KEYS and board, accepted E033 Match._finish/_board] A watched or competitive result keeps the original's ten statistics, two raw bars, man of the match, half-time score and substitutions. A quick result keeps only the score and the goals.
  - [designed: screen 08] The possession and territory bars show raw, "meaning unverified".
- **Owns**: `goal2/game/match_report.py` (new), `goal2/desktop/fixtures_page.py` (the result section, `open_result`, `continue_after_result`, `refresh_result`), `goal2/desktop/window.py` (`match_finished` after each completion), `goal2/desktop/style.py`, `goal2/tests/test_match_result_t97.py` (new), `docs/desktop-management.md`.
- **Scope**:
  - Every completed match (quick, watched or competitive) opens its result on **Fixtures & match**, with **Continue** focused. The result shows:
    - FULL TIME and the score;
    - the kind, half time and engine;
    - each side's scorers;
    - the statistics, plus regulation, extra time and penalties for a competitive match;
    - man of the match and the substitutions.

    What the record does not keep says "Not recorded", never an invented value. Names go through the game's name table, so renames and localized pools carry into the result.
  - **Round results** opens the existing table.
  - **Continue** returns to the Country desk with **Prepare match** focused for the next fixture.
  - A preview shows no result. Reading a result changes nothing.
- **Done when**:
  1. `python3 -m unittest goal2.tests.test_match_result_t97 -v`, on a real original friendly played in the test, shows each of the following:
     - The report equals the record: teams, score, half time, scorers, the ten statistics in the original's order, the bars, man of the match and the substitutions.
     - A quick result reports only what it keeps, and a renamed scorer carries into the result.
     - The window opens the result with Continue focused after quick and watched completions. Round results and Continue work, and Continue leads to Prepare match.
     - A preview shows no result, and the state is unchanged.
  2. The retained desktop scenarios pass.
  3. `python3 tools/check_premerge.py --expected-head <sha>` passes.
- **Review**: none (presentation over validated records).
- **Hazards**:
  - Never present an absent statistic as a value.
  - Never replay a job on Continue.
