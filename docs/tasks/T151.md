  vendored tables of T150; there is no Unicorn fallback (owner, 2026-10-31). The outcome goal2 keeps (`WatchedOutcome.memory`) stays a

  writes (upstream T57 write watch), comes from the prepared image. Bench answers, pauses, pitch
  snapshots and reports work as before. Out of scope: the tournament continuation between matches
  (goal2-archaeology #427) stays the original's under Unicorn; the quick engine; the interpreter (T152).
  The original engine stays reachable only as the comparison arm of the tests.

     reaching extra time or a shoot-out when the templates allow), `_drive_match` on the port gives the
     same result, events (the board screen's text excepted, as upstream compares), seeds and memory
     (outside the video memory) as on the original engine; the port run imports no `unicorn`.
