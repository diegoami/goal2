- **Scope**: The desk replaces T163's placeholder home: after a new career or a load, the player lands on S06. The
  task protects one claim: **from the desk the player reaches everything the original's hub offers, and chooses
  the eleven.**
  1. **S06, the manager's desk.**
     - It shows the manager (name; a rating reads *unknown*, as the mockup does, where the route has none), the
       team with world and continental rankings, and the next opponents with their rankings and the match's kind.
     - It shows the managers, the twenty with role codes (the eleven above the bench), and the six options:
       team info, fixture list, tournament, history, select squad (Space), play next game.
     - The cursor starts on *team info*, ↑↓ moves it, and Enter opens the option.
     - **T** opens S10; **Space** opens S08; **F1** help.
     - **Placeholders for later tasks:** *fixture list*, *tournament* and *history* open T178's screens (#560). Until those
       exist, each says which task brings it. *play next game* says the match screens come with T167. **F3/F4**
       come with T167/T168, and **F7/F10** with T168. Each placeholder names the task and does nothing else.
     - **Re-import data** (decision 14) opens S01 over the data folder.
  2. **S07, team info.** S04's card for any country, with its list, the next opponent marked. Arrows choose the
     country, Ins switches the ranking, Space shows its twenty (T165's card, read-only). **T** shows its tactics,
     Enter its fixtures (a placeholder until T178), Esc returns to the desk.
  3. **#555, the squad swap.** On S08, Enter on a squad player and then Enter on another squad player swaps them.
     The eleven and the bench follow the original's order, and the result is confirmed through the model as
     today's Team swap does. An unavailable starter (injured, suspended) can be moved to the bench this way.
  4. **#559, S04's list.**
     - The countries are ordered by the ranking Ins selects, world or continental, as the original's capture shows.
     - Holland's average age at the capture's start year equals the capture's 28. If it does not, find why: a stop
       and report is acceptable when the cause is a rule the route lacks.
  5. Mouse equals key everywhere, and Esc from every screen the desk opens returns to the desk.
