- **Scope**: the original's goal replay, saved goals and saved summaries in the new window. The task protects
  one claim: **a replay shows exactly the pitch samples the engine sent for that goal, and a saved goal or
  summary shows again exactly what was saved.**
  1. **The goal questions (S18).** After a goal in a watched match, S18 asks *action replay?* (**Y**/**N**). Y
     plays the goal's samples again on S17, with *REPLAY* where *R result* is. Then *save this goal?*
     (**Y**/**N**), and Y asks its name in S21's box.
     - With *automatic action replay* on (S15's Ins), the replay plays without the question, as the original.
     - A quick result (R) asks nothing.
     - The match waits during the questions and the replay: no sample is lost, and the pacing (LEAD) holds.
  2. **The goal's samples.** S17 keeps a bounded window of the samples it has shown, enough to cover the goal
     (name the length from T156-R1's replays, or `[designed]` with a reason). The replay redraws those samples;
     it never asks the engine again.
  3. **Saved goals.** goal2's own format (the original's goal files are not read): the samples, the two countries,
     the score and minute, the scorer. They live in the per-user data folder, beside the saves. **F3** on the
     desk lists them by name; Enter plays one on S17 (through S15, as the original), Esc back.
  4. **The match summary (S20).** After a watched match's full time, S20 asks *save the match summary?*
     (**Y**/**N**), then a name. The summary is S20's board as shown. **F4** on the desk lists the saved
     summaries; Enter shows one on S20, Esc back.
  5. Remove the desk's F3/F4 placeholders.
