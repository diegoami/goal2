# T99 Team tactics and the attribute table in the GOAL style

- **Kind**: slice (#304, the first-release Team page below the card)
- **Issue**: #304 · **Branch**: `task/T99-team-tactics` locally (a cloud session uses its own branch) · **Merge after**: T98
- **Evidence**:
  - [designed: owner playtest 2026-10-21, #304] The tactics checkboxes and the raw table "do not look like GOAL at all".
  - [designed: accepted vision #266 screen 03] The Team page is the roster and the full card, ending in the routes **Pool / Compare**, **Attribute table** and **Team tactics**.
  - [designed: accepted vision #266 screen 16] **Team tactics** has:
    - eight categories, each with its current value word: Defence system, Protection, Offside, Positioning, Possession, Passing, Tackling, Marking;
    - Enter to change the selected tactic;
    - the XI, the manager and the captain on the left;
    - **Confirm plan** and **Discard / Team**.
  - [confirmed: `docs/original-game/images/m244-team-tactics.png`; the tactic bits of `window.TACTICS`] Element k is bit `1<<(k-1)` of `country_tactics`, and its words are TACTICS' two alternatives: unchecked, then checked. The categories pair with the elements as 5 Defence system, 6 Protection, 7 Offside, 1 Positioning, 2 Possession, 3 Passing, 4 Tackling, 8 Marking. Elements 1–4 are the card's position, possession, passing and tackling (`game/player_card.TACTICS`).
  - [derived: `goal2/game/country_card.py`] The formation is counted from the eleven's positions; nothing is stored.

- **Scope**:
  [designed: owner, 2026-10-21, on the first T99 build: "no tables, no numbers when you can have
  bars, no lifeless list of players, copy the style of goal2"] The original's squad and
  team-tactics screens govern over screens 03 and 16
  ([confirmed: `docs/original-game/images/m244-squad.png`, `m244-team-tactics.png`, M244-G1A-02]).
  - **Roster strip.** On every Team view, the roster is a strip on the right, under the country
    name, as in the original:
    - the eleven, a rule, then the nine on the bench;
    - each row shows the initial and name in the role colour, and the role letter on the right;
    - there is no +/- prefix;
    - the selected row is highlighted, and an unavailable player is marked;
    - Up/Down select a player, and Space moves the selected player between the XI and the bench
      (the existing toggle).
  - **Card view** (view 0, left of the strip). The full card shows:
    - the twelve skills as bars with no numbers;
    - each characteristic as a bar with its descriptor word inside, as in the original.

    Below the card are the routes **Pool / Compare >**, **Team tactics >** and **Rename (F2)**.
  - **Team tactics view** (view 1, left of the strip). It keeps the TEAM DEFAULTS rows as built.
    The left card shows the team, the manager, the captain (`window.captain`, restyled) and the
    counted formation. It carries no text list of players: the strip shows them.
    **Confirm plan** and **Discard / Team** finish there.
  - **No table.** There is no attribute table view. `window.table` remains the internal model of
    the XI checks and is never shown. Escape returns to the card view.
  - **Unchanged.** `window.tactics` (the (element, button) pairs, in the order 5,6,7,1,2,3,4,8),
    the footer draft bar, `draft()`, Confirm, Discard, the tactic bits, renames, and every save.
