- **Scope**: the match stops that T167 left on a default answer become the original's screens. The task protects
  one claim: **every substitution the player makes on S19 is the pair the engine receives, and every refusal
  the player sees is the engine's.**
  1. **S18, match stop.** A box over the pitch with the event, the score and minute:
     - *SUBSTITUTION OPPORTUNITY* (your player injured): any key opens S15, as the original. From S15, *players*
       opens S19 in *make substitutions now* mode.
     - The other side's substitution: a notice; any key plays on.
     - The penalty shoot-out's start: a notice before T167's shoot-out order (S16).
     - The goal questions (*action replay?*, *save this goal?*) are T181's; S18 is built so T181 adds them.
  2. **S19, the bench.**
     - The card of the highlighted player; the eleven and the bench; the mode as the original names it (*make
       substitutions now*, or *substitution requested* during play); **substitutions left**.
     - **↑↓** player; **Enter** takes off or puts on, in either order, and the hint names which. In request mode,
       Enter requests or cancels.
     - **R**, **C** positions, as today's bench (`CoreBench.reposition`) and S08.
     - **T** the highlighted player's tactics (S09), with the mark line (scope 3).
     - **Esc** back to S15 (at a break) or to the pitch (during play). At the stoppage after a pause, Esc says
       the requested substitutions are still made (#316).
     - A refused substitution shows the engine's reason and waits for a key. With none left, "no more
       substitutes".
     - **During play:** Esc or Enter on S17 requests the bench (`pause`), as T167. At the next stoppage S19 opens
       in request mode. The pairs go through `answer` once; the engine's report is shown.
     - **At half time and extra time:** S20's Esc and S15's *players* open S19 in *make substitutions now* mode.
  3. **S09's mark line.** With a match set up (from S19's T, or S15's *players* then T), S09 shows the fifth
     line, *mark*, with the opponent's players, as the original (`1771:49B8`). The mark is the answer the engine
     receives. After the match the marks are cleared, as the original.
  4. **BM-7 for watched friendlies.** A watched friendly plays with BM-7's unlimited budget, as the Core's new
     careers do (`owner-substitutions-v1`). Pass the budget explicitly: a friendly's S19 counter must not read
     the original's five. If the engine's `substitutions` cannot express "unlimited", use the Core's 255 and cite
     it.
  5. Remove `DEFAULTED`, `BENCH_LATER` and S20's "T180" note. No stop keeps a default answer except where the
     original itself plays on.

    shown.
  - **Answering twice.** Each stop is answered once, in the order the engine asked.
