    cell, no interior padding; **a frame is a rule with its lowercase label sitting on it, the rule continuing as

  line-art titles and signature elements (#673–#680), the pitch (T179).

     it and dashes to the edge.

- **Hazards**: a screen that overflowed at T209's 32 px is solved by layout (fewer rows, a new panel), per screen,
  never by a smaller size. Qt style-sheet padding and borders are the usual off-grid offenders.
