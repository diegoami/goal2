  1. **S11 Fixtures.**
     - The coming round with each fixture's kind, ✓ on your own match, ↑↓ scroll, Esc back to the desk.
     - Enter on the highlighted row does what the design names:
       - your own match: *watch match*, which is always on (owner decision 1);
       - another country's match: *watch match* or *auto result*, as the original's ✓ toggle (E031 C17–C18);
       - your own friendly: *cancel or rearrange*.
     - **Watching another country's match** needs the engine to play a computer-vs-computer fixture watched.
       Check that `engine_server` and `prepare_watched` accept such a fixture. If they do, the ✓ is stored in the
       career and honoured when the round is played (T167 plays it). If they do not, stop and report. Do not
       build a toggle that does nothing.
     - **Cancel or rearrange** your own friendly: build it only if the route has a call for it. Otherwise the row
       says it comes later with an issue, and the PR names it.
  2. **S12 Tournament.**
     - The tabs (Qualifying, Finals, Championship) and the continent (Space).
     - The managed group's table (P W D L F A T) beside its matches, from `competition_table`. The other groups and
       continents come from the route's world state, where it has them.
     - Finals and Championship say *not reached yet* until the route plays them (T170).
     - ↑↓ group, ←→ display, Esc back to the desk.
  3. **S13 History.** This career's results, from `campaign.results`, laid out as the design's results-by-season
