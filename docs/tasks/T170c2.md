# T170c2 Publish a completed sealed round atomically into the campaign

- **Kind**: feature (m3 Track B, #509; a mergeable leaf of [T170](T170.md), #566)
- **Issue**: #566 · **Branch**: `task/T170c2-atomic-publication` · **Merge after**: T170c1 (merged, #656)
- **Evidence**:
  - [designed: main, 2026-11-08; T170 "Mergeable implementation leaves"] After the headless
    coordinator (T170b, #654) and the retained-Match consumer (T170c1, #656), the next bounded
    contract is **atomic campaign publication**. T170c1 states its own exclusion: "No engine
    copies, wire/schema, publication, campaign or UI changes." Nothing publishes a sealed round
    today.
  - [confirmed: `goal2/game/season.py`] `SeasonSession` owns the accepted state, the verified
    Tables, the persistent Keys and the pending Match, and exposes `observe()`, `advance()` and
    `resume(match)` as detached observations with call number, status, canonical input/output
    hashes, fixture/news deltas and both final RNG words. It publishes nothing.
  - [confirmed: `goal2/game/watched_publication.py`] `WatchedPublication` already has the shape
    this needs: `begin(fixture)`, `prepare_completion(token, outcome)`, `publish_prepared(token,
    preparation)`, `complete(token, outcome)`, `cancel()`, with an owner check and a token.
    Reuse its two-phase semantics; do not invent a second publication path.
- **Owns**:
  - `goal2/game/season.py` only where a completed call is handed to publication, or a new
    `goal2/game/season_publication.py` if that keeps `SeasonSession` free of campaign imports.
  - `goal2/tests/test_season_publication_t170c2.py`.
- **Scope**: A completed round of the sealed season is published into the campaign in one atomic
  step, or not at all. The task protects one claim: **the campaign either holds the whole of a
  completed round or none of it, and never holds it twice.**
  1. **Two phases, as the watched path has them.** Preparation reads the session's completed
     observation and builds the campaign candidate; publication installs it. A failure, an
     exception or a cancellation between the two leaves the campaign byte-for-byte as it was.
  2. **Exactly once.** Publishing the same completed call twice is refused, not applied twice.
     The refusal names the call. A rejected or pending call publishes nothing.
  3. **The session stays the owner of its state.** Publication reads the observation's canonical
     hashes; it does not reach into the Match, construct a backend, replay a prefix, call a tail,
     or mutate private options. T170c1's and T170b's purity rules continue to hold.
  4. **No UI and no wire.** The window, `engine_server.py`, the schema and the watched/competitive
     jobs are untouched. The UI leaf follows separately.
  5. **No new campaign rules.** Results, records, tables, calendars, ordered news and RNG are the
     session's; this leaf moves them, it does not compute them.

## Done when

1. `python3 -m unittest goal2.tests.test_season_publication_t170c2` fails before and passes after.
   It shows, with no network and no Qt:
   - a completed call publishes once, and the campaign's canonical hash afterwards equals the
     observation's declared output hash;
   - publishing the same call again is refused and changes nothing;
   - a failure raised between preparation and publication leaves the campaign's canonical hash
     equal to its value before preparation;
   - a cancelled preparation publishes nothing;
   - a pending or rejected call publishes nothing and is refused by name.
2. `python3 -m unittest goal2.tests.test_season_t170b goal2.tests.test_owned_match_driver_t170c1`
   passes unchanged: this leaf weakens none of their purity controls. The PR names any assertion
   that moved, and why.
3. `python3 tools/check_provenance.py` passes.
4. Main-owned: `python3 tools/check_premerge.py --expected-head <sha>` and required CI. The
   implementer runs its own modules only and leaves full suites to CI (process.md §10).

- **Review**: owner's m3 milestone review; per-PR only if requested.
- **Hazards**:
  - **A second publication path.** `WatchedPublication` exists; reuse its two-phase shape rather
    than writing a parallel one that can drift from it.
  - **Partial writes.** The atomicity claim is the whole point: build the candidate completely
    before anything is installed, and prove it with the mid-flight failure case.
  - **Scope creep into T129p.** Pre-match policy, the pool view and automatic XI belong to the
    upstream contracts (#703/#704), which another session owns. Do not touch them.
  - This leaf does not complete #566: the integrated two-season Core comparison and the UI
    lifecycle remain, and required T183 follows them.
