- **Scope**: The new window plays your own friendly watched on S17, in the main window, from the desk. The task
  protects one claim: **what S17 shows is the engine's samples and events, in order, paced by S17's own
  acknowledgements, and the match the player watched is the match the engine published.**
  1. **S17.**
     - The pitch: the original's pitch lines and surround, each player's role code coloured by side, the ball.
     - The score, minute, match kind and the last two events. The commentary is always beside the pitch.
     - **←→** speed (the six `SPEEDS`); **↑↓** display (marks); **Space** enlarges the commentary.
     - **R** quick result: the engine plays on without pacing, and the screen says *HANG ON A TICK* as the
       original does (`t156-267-quick-hang-on`).
     - **F9**: goal2 has no sound. The key says so and does nothing else; the PR lists it as a departure.
     - **Esc / Enter** request the bench (a pause) as the original. The bench screen is T167's, so until then a
       granted pause is answered with no substitution, and the screen says the bench comes with T167.
  2. **Pacing.** S17 acknowledges each sample once it is drawn, so the engine never runs more than LEAD ahead
     (T102's rule, kept by the bridge).
  3. **Decisions until T167.** Half time, substitution offers and the other stops are answered with the defaults
     today's controller uses for an unanswered stop. The screen notes each stop (*half time*, …) in the
     commentary. T167 replaces this with S18–S20.
  4. **The interim route.** Until T167 brings the round, the match menu and the captain, *play next game* on the
     desk starts the next own **friendly** watched on S17, with the original's suggested captain (as T143 picks
     it). At full time it publishes through the bridge and returns to the desk, with the result in the desk's
     status line. A qualifier says it comes with T176 and T167. This makes the opening stage's friendlies
     playable in the new window now.
  5. **Closing the window during a match** cancels it through the bridge, publishes nothing, and leaves no engine
     process behind.
