# T101 Screens fit without scrolling: one fixed page, scaled to the window

- **Kind**: slice (#312; the rule for every later screen, G10–G14 of #308)
- **Issue**: #312 · **Branch**: `task/T101-fixed-page` locally (a cloud session uses its own branch) · **Merge after**: T100
- **Evidence**:
  - [designed: owner, 2026-10-22, #312] "In many of the screens you have to scroll, but Goal2 managed to make everything fit on the screen without scrolling." The owner chose the following:
    - **A fixed page, scaled.** Each screen is composed once for a fixed page of 1440×900, and the whole page scales to the window, as the pitch view already does. Nothing scrolls at any size; a small window shows everything smaller.
    - **Now, then every new screen.**
  - [confirmed: `docs/original-game/images/*`] Every original screen is one 80×25 text page.
  - [designed: T94] The pitch view already scales its page to the window (`pygame_viewer.layout`).
  - This supersedes the small-window design of accepted screens 11 and 17 and T90/T91: the `NARROW_SHELL` collapse, `Columns` reflow, page scroll areas and pane routes.
- **Owns**:
  - `goal2/desktop/` (the shell, the pages, the launch window, `style.py`);
  - `goal2/tests/test_fixed_page_t101.py` (new);
  - the redesign and desktop scenarios' size and scroll contracts in `tools/desktop-ui/`;
  - `docs/desktop-management.md`;
  - the decision in `docs/redesign-253/vision-reconciliation.md`.
- **Scope**:
  - **The page.** The main window and the launch window each show one page of 1440×900 logical pixels.
    - The window scales the page uniformly to fit, keeps its aspect, and fills the margin black. The page is never cut and never scrolled.
    - Keyboard, mouse, focus, drop-downs, dialogs and accessible names work at every scale.
    - The way is the implementer's (for example a `QGraphicsView` holding the page, or a scale applied to the whole page); `docs/desktop-management.md` says which, and why.
  - **Every page fits it.** These pages are recomposed until their content fits 1440×900 with no scroll area:
    - Country desk, Team (card and tactics views), Pool, Fixtures & match (preparation, result, half time) and Save;
    - the launch window's pages.

    Content is never dropped for space: a part that cannot fit becomes a route, as the original does with its separate screens.
  - **Superseded and removed:** the side bar's narrow-window collapse, `Columns` reflow and the page scroll bars. F11 and the retractable bars stay.
  - **The no-scroll check.** A shared helper, used by this task's test and available to every later screen task, asserts at a given window size that:
    - no scroll bar is visible;
    - every visible widget lies inside the page;
    - no widget is clipped by its parent.
- **Done when**:
  1. `python3 -m unittest goal2.tests.test_fixed_page_t101 -v` shows that the no-scroll check passes for every page and Team view, and for the launch window. It covers:
     - window sizes 1440×900, 1050×780, 900×640 and 2560×1600;
     - Qt scale 1 and 2 (`QT_SCALE_FACTOR` in a child process).

     It also shows that the page's scale follows the window, with the aspect kept, and that a click and a key reach the intended widget at 900×640.
  2. The retained desktop scenarios pass: redesign shell, overview, squad and pool, squad, tactics-labels, country-selection, save-decisions, competitive-play, watched and launch. Their size and scroll contracts move to the fixed page, and no check is dropped.
  3. `python3 tools/check_premerge.py --expected-head <sha>` passes.
- **Review**: none (layout; no game state is touched).
- **Hazards**:
  - Never shrink text below what the scaled page gives, and never hide content to make a page fit: make a route.
  - Pop-ups (drop-downs, dialogs) must appear at the scaled position.
