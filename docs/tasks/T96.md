  - [confirmed: `0791:9372` (code/seg_0791_15.asm:795-821)] **Up/Down** step the mark style `DS:066A` back and forward over 0..4. It starts at **1**.
  - [confirmed: `0791:05C6`, `0791:0691` (named/seg_0791_01.c:357-381)] The five styles:
    - 0 ☺;
    - 1 the role code `DS:69A8`;
    - 2 an arrow `DS:06D4[(slot < 12) xor (DS:831B > DS:8319)]` (◄, ►);
    - 3 `DS:06D2[slot < 12]` (A, H);
    - 4 the digit `(slot − 1) % 11 + 0x2F`.

    Keepers (slots 1 and 12) are always ☺. A ☺ on the slot on the ball (`DS:830B`) is drawn ☻ (seg_0791_07.c:550-557).
  - [confirmed: `0791:A3B6` (code/seg_0791_17.asm:151)] The referee's glyph is `0x0D01`, a light-magenta ☺, at `DS:82EE`. The two linesmen are the sprites at `DS:82E2 + 6·side`, with the same glyph. They run the touchlines (x = ±8) at the offside line (`0791:8C67`, `0791:8CF5`). All three are drawn after the players.
  - [confirmed: `DS:0883`; `0791:8537`; `0791:8F10`] The 22 slots are drawn in the order 22, 11, 1, 12, 6, 17, 2, 13, 21, 10, 18, 7, 3, 20, 9, 14, 5, 16, 4, 15, 8, 19. The last drawn wins a shared cell. The ball shows only on an empty cell.
  - [confirmed: `0791:9372` (named/seg_0791_08.c:170-178)] The speed byte runs 0..5: six speeds. [designed: owner, 2026-10-21 "Six, as the code"] Slowest 10, Slower 15, Slow 30, Normal 60, Fast 120, Fastest 240 samples per second.
