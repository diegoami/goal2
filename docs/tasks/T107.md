- **Scope**:
  - **The views** read from memory, each field cited:
    - the managed squad in the original's order (eleven, then bench) with role codes;
    - each player's card: name, age, caps, goals, height, foot, positions, cards, skills as bars (F008's derivation is the original's own; read or recompute by the cited routine), characteristics, tactics and mark;
    - the team tactics byte, with its eight value words;
    - the pool of the current manager (F011).
  - **The driver's team actions.** Each is the original's own keys from the manager menu and back, closed-loop, and refuses keys a screen does not list:
    - `swap(a, b)` within the squad (eleven ⇄ bench, or reorder);
    - `exchange(squad_player, pool_player)` with the pool, where the original allows it, with the original's refusal shown otherwise;
    - `set_position(player, code)` (R/C);
    - `set_tactic(element, on)` on team tactics;
    - `set_own_tactic(player, element, value)` and `set_marking(player, opponent)`;
    - `rename(player, text)` (F2).
  - **The desktop.** On the core route, Team and Pool are enabled. They are T99/T101's pages filled from the core's views: the roster strip, the card and the tactic rows. Each edit runs the driver at once, so the page always shows what the original holds. The footer draft and Confirm of the Python route are not used on the core route, because the original has no draft. A refused action shows the original's message.
  - **Moving through the original's screens.** The desktop never shows the original's own screens. The driver passes through them out of sight.
