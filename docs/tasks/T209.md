# T209 The foundation, part 1: a 16 × 32 cell, one text size, 4:3

- **Kind**: UI foundation (first of two; T210 is the frames and the grid-snapping)
- **Issue**: #667 · **Branch**: `task/T209-cell` · **Merge after**: T208 (merged)
- **Evidence**:
  - [designed: owner, 2026-11-08; #666] The decisions in `docs/design/ui-2026-11/fidelity-review.md` §3: the
    cell becomes **16 × 32** and the page **90 × 28** at 1440×900; **one text size everywhere**, anything
    larger drawn as line-art (#673–#675); the page presented at **4:3** (1.2 vertical stretch, letterboxed,
    never re-flowed). This supersedes #598 (24 px) in the direction it went. The approved screens, flow and keys
    (#526) are not reopened.
  - [confirmed: measured 2026-11-09, `QFont` on the vendored `PxPlus IBM VGA 8x16`, offscreen Qt] pixel size 32
    gives advance 16 and line height 32 (24 px gives 12 and 24: off the grid). A stretch works on this font:
    pixel size 38 with `QFont.setStretch(83)` gives advance 16 and height 39, so a taller glyph does not widen.
  - Today: `goal2/ui/theme.py` `CELL = 16`, `TEXT = 24`, `HEADING_SIZE = 2 * CELL`; `start_look.py` `BIG`, `HUGE`;
    `goal2/desktop/fixed_page.py` scales the page uniformly (`WIDTH, HEIGHT = 1440, 900`).
- **Owns**: `goal2/ui/theme.py`, `goal2/ui/start_look.py`, `goal2/desktop/fixed_page.py`, the three tests that
  name the old sizes (`test_ui_foundation_t162.py`, `test_readable_ui_t186.py`, `test_substitution_ui_636.py`),
  a new `goal2/tests/test_ui_cell_t209.py`, `docs/design/ui-2026-11/README.md` §3, the layout receipt
  `docs/design/ui-2026-11/t186-layout-receipt.json` (regenerated by `tools/ui_readability_screenshots.py`,
  never by hand), and the `T1*-screens/` renders.
- **Scope**: `CELL` becomes 16 × 32 and `TEXT` 32; `HEADING_SIZE`, `BIG` and `HUGE` go, and what used them is
  drawn at `TEXT` (colour and position carry the hierarchy). The logical page is 90 × 28 cells (1440 × 896).
  `FixedPage` presents it at 4:3: vertical lengths scale by 1.2 × s and horizontal ones by s, the text by
  `QFont` stretch, centred on black. The one claim: **every glyph on every screen is one size, on a 16 × 32 grid,
  and the page keeps 4:3 at any window size.** Out of scope: panel edges and frames (T210), line-art titles.
  **Screens that overflow at 32 px are a finding.** Report them per screen (`fixed_page.problems`); never shrink
  a size, clip, or drop a value to hide one.
- **Done when**:
  1. `python3 -m unittest goal2.tests.test_ui_cell_t209` passes, and fails on `main`: the body font advance is 16
     and its line height 32; the page is at least 88 columns by 27 rows at 1440×900; no screen's text-bearing
     widget reports a pixel size other than `theme.TEXT` (scaled); `HEADING_SIZE`, `BIG` and `HUGE` appear
     nowhere under `goal2/`.
  2. The same module asserts the page's rect is 4:3 within 0.5 % at 1440×900, 1280×720, 800×600 and 1920×1080
     (letterboxed, never re-flowed), and that a glyph's advance to line-height ratio stays 1 : 2 × 1.2 within a
     pixel of rounding.
  3. `python3 tools/ui_readability_screenshots.py <dir>` regenerates the receipt; it says `normal_text_px: 32`
     and lists every screen whose `problems` are not empty, which is posted on #667.
  4. `python3 docs/design/ui-2026-11/check_mapping.py` exits 0; `check_density.py` passes the re-rendered hub and
     squad at the default floor (other screens at their counterpart's own minimum, `fidelity-review.md` §2.2).
  5. `docs/design/ui-2026-11/README.md` §3 states the cell, the grid, the one-size rule and 4:3, cites the
     owner's decision of 2026-11-08 and says it supersedes #598.
  6. `python3 tools/check_premerge.py --expected-head <sha>` passes.
- **Hazards**: a style sheet cannot carry a font stretch, so the stretch is set on the application and widget
  fonts in code, and a widget that re-sets its own font loses it: test it per screen. Pixel rounding at a
  fractional scale can make the advance 15 or 17: assert the ratio, not a constant. `pitch.py` and the roster draw
  their own text in page coordinates: they take the same size and the same stretch.
