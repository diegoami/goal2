- **Scope**:
  - **The worker asks.** At a `half_time`, `pause` or `substitution` decision, the worker reports it and waits for the answer from the parent. The answer is a list of `(off, on)` pairs; an empty list means play on.
    - `extra_time` and `shootout_order` keep today's default answers, unchanged.
    - The return channel is a private, bounded journal written only by the parent, with the same rules as the events journal (mode-0700 folder, length-prefixed, token-checked).
    - A non-watched caller of `_drive_match` (`run_watched_match`, tests) keeps the play-on default unless it passes a policy.
  - **Pacing.** The worker never runs more than a declared number of frames ahead of the frames the viewer has acknowledged. Then a mid-play Enter applies within that lead of what the player sees. `docs/desktop-management.md` states the lead and why.
  - **The pitch view.** When playback reaches a decision, the view freezes on it and shows the original's board text ("HALF TIME", or the stoppage).
    - **Enter** in play asks for the bench. The viewer sends it to the parent, which calls `pause()`. The hint becomes `[Enter] Bench`, and **Space** stays the commentary.
    - Esc keeps its current meanings.
  - **The bench.** It is a fixed-page view of the main window (T101), raised in front when a decision arrives. It copies the original's in-match players screen:
    - the eleven and the bench in the roster strip, with injured players marked;
    - the selected player's card;
    - **substitutions left** from the decision's `substitutions_left`, never a constant;
    - the prompt "take off this player" or "put on this player";
    - **Play** sends the chosen pairs, and Esc with nothing chosen plays on.

    A pair the engine refuses shows the original's message, and the counter follows the engine. At a pause, the chosen pairs are a request: the view says that they are made at the next stoppage. The `substitution` decision that follows offers them again.
  - **After the match**, the substitute stays in the eleven in the published state, as the original keeps the squad words (F029). Nothing new is invented for the save.

- **Hazards**:
  - Never let the desktop decide whether a substitution is legal. Pass the pair, and show what the engine made or refused. The counter comes from the engine.
  - Answering a decision is the only way to touch the match. Never write a byte into the image.
  - The worker is ahead of the view. Without the pacing, a mid-play Enter lands minutes late, so prove the lead in a test.
  - A worker blocked on an answer must still cancel within the #254 contract (`cancel_and_reap`). Closing the viewer while the bench is open cancels the match, as it does today.
