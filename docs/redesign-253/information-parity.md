# Information parity contract

[designed: #253] The proposal allocates every evidenced original field and significant state to a decision context. **A mapped destination is a design commitment, not proof that the rebuild implements the information.** Missing source projections and unsupported interactions remain delivery dependencies. An unavailable value must read “Not recorded” or “Not supported in this match” with a specific reason; it cannot count as achieved parity. The complete original-information release gate requires resolving the relevant dependency, or an explicit owner decision acknowledging the remaining difference.

[confirmed: accepted manual `ebdd6879d469a574f6d4b446f59b33a6be3ffd81`; E031/M244 references in rows] The independently inspectable [source inventory](parity/source-fields.tsv) contains234 field/state rows. The [destination allocation](parity/destinations.tsv) maps those IDs; [shared decision contexts](parity/contexts.tsv) say when/why, discoverability, delivery owner and limitations. The rendered matrices below present those same allocations for human review. The [64-image inspection ledger](evidence/screens-inspected.tsv) records each visually examined source, hash, visible details and journey trace; `dos-shell.png` is explicitly environment-only.

[designed: #253] Read the three matrices in player order:

1. [Launch, country, squad, attributes and tactics](parity/management.md).
2. [Fixtures, competition, country records and campaign news](parity/campaign.md).
3. [Preparation, live decisions, results, saves and retrospective play](parity/match.md).

[designed: #253] Destination keys are defined in the [screen hierarchy](journeys.md#screen-hierarchy). “Direct” means visible in the relevant decision surface without leaving it. Selecting a roster row may change the card, but does not commit the player to the XI. “1 action” means a named tab/button with persistent team/fixture context and a return path; it does not mean an undocumented shortcut or a tooltip. At small windows, a labelled card pane may replace side-by-side columns; the selection and pending action remain visible. Critical readiness, selected identities, clock/score/phase and primary action never depend on horizontal page scrolling.

[designed: #253] A complete player card exposes all12 skills and5 characteristics together at the normal desktop size. Optional compact tables retain T84's seven-column default and38/37 expanded columns. Showing only Ability, Energy or Confidence does not satisfy the original card. Current national role and club role, personal and team tactics, fitness and energy, original morale and rebuild Confidence, normal goals and shoot-out kicks remain distinct. The comparison view pins both outgoing and incoming identities/cards before an exchange.

[confirmed: manual coverage; E031 sources] Original unknowns remain unknown: country-bar units, original performance/card-box encodings, the trailing P, exact pitch glyphs/F9 suffix, title/display semantics, no-substitutions-left case and several unpictured branches. F008/T84 can separately establish a supported numeric player-skill projection; it does not establish every original verbal bucket. [designed] The product can explain a verified rebuild measure, clearly labelled as such, beside the original category; it cannot quietly substitute one meaning for another.

[designed: #253] Validation compares the independent source inventory to allocations, checks destination/owner and every screenshot/journey trace, and rejects deleted fields, blank destinations or a removed approval gate. It is a traceability check. The independent reviewer still judges whether the inventory actually matches the manual and whether the proposed layout is usable; a green script cannot decide that.
