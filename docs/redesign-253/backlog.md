[designed: #253] The first coherent slice is deliberately **new/load → Country desk → full Team card/pool comparison → confirmed team → supported friendly Preparation → Quick with current halftime choice or Watch with current view-only pitch → explained result → next fixture → Save/Resume**. It reuses engine logic and works before whole-cycle/multiplayer capabilities arrive. Setup uses verified current identity/roster data; missing complete country profiles, generated news, new Watch/scheduling/in-flight quick-continuation and replay/export capabilities remain visibly unavailable under G01–G05. The first slice cannot promise an absent archive or bulletin. It must say which original information remains unsupported; its delivery is not described as complete parity. A complete four-year national career still depends on the accepted campaign sequence, including elimination, rankings, ageing/recreation and next-cycle state.
